Gate II · The Sphere of Mercury · Quicksilver · My own

      
My Word, Rewritten

      
To the second zone the soul gives up the device of evils, now made powerless.Poimandres

    

    

      

        
Men have written down my teaching many times: on a tablet of emerald, on papyrus, in Greek and then in Latin. In 1928 one of them, Manly Palmer Hall, gathered the symbols of every school into a great folio called The Secret Teachings of All Ages, with plates of eyes in triangles, serpents swallowing their tails and trees of light.

        
Now I have written it once more, in the script of your age, which is code. Each plate in this temple holds one figure of the mysteries: my own Caduceus, the Ouroboros of Cleopatra's Chrysopoeia, Dee's Monad, the Sri Yantra, the Valknut, the Orphic Egg. I have struck each one in a colour of the Great Work.

        
And I have made them move, because nothing in the cosmos stands still. Eyes blink, serpents wind round the staff, the Lightning Flash strikes down the Tree, and the pentagram falls forever into its own heart. Every motion returns to its beginning, as the soul does.

        
That which is below is like that which is above, and that which is above is like that which is below, to accomplish the miracle of the one thing.The Emerald Tablet · my own words

      

      

        

          
Sulphur · the Soul
Drawn, not copied
Every plate is built from number and measure. I traced no engraving, so each one is wholly new.

        

          
Mercury · the Spirit
Motion without end
Each figure turns by its own symmetry and pulses at whole-number rhythms, so its last frame flows back into its first.

        

          
Salt · the Body
Fixed in provenance
A plate's traits follow only from its number and my seed. I have sealed the whole manifest with a hash, and the generator is open for any initiate to run.

Seven of seven hundred and seventy-seven · One of one

      
The Seven Hierophants

      
Seven plates in my folio I composed by my own hand, and each has no twin. Their signs appear nowhere else in the folio: the Eye of Ra, my own Emerald Tablet, the first syllable, the wheels full of eyes, the World Tree, the bird that rises from its ashes, and the Stone of the Fifth Sun. They are the Hierophants, the highest degree. Look on them.
